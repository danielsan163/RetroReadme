# Changelog

## v0.2.0

- New guide: Kirby's Dream Land 3 (SNES). All 30 Heart Stars stage by stage, plus Zero, MG-5, Boss Butch and Jumping (34 on the checklist), which friends and abilities each Heart Star needs, bosses, sub-games and hints.
- Launcher: new theme menu (Select, or D-pad up to the top bar) with a color scheme for each console: NES, FC, SNES, SFC, Genesis, GBA, GB DMG and PS1. Moving through the list previews each theme live; A keeps it, B puts the saved one back. Your pick is remembered.
- Launcher: games are listed alphabetically under each platform heading.
- Launcher: each console heading is now a row you can collapse or expand with A, with its guides nested underneath. Collapsed consoles stay collapsed next time you open the app.
- Wario Land 4: each level now lists its jewel pieces, CD and Keyzer in the order you reach them (separately for Normal and Hard), with the frog switch shown in its place so you know what to grab before the escape.
- New guide: Metroid: Zero Mission (GBA). All 100 items by area with a Jump to area menu, items that need Chozodia gear flagged, the upgrade route, full boss strategies (with each boss's weak point), techniques and hints.
- New guide: Castlevania: Aria of Sorrow (GBA). All 120 souls with effects, areas and the best farming spots, a Jump to soul type menu, souls by area, the key-soul route, endings and farming hints.

## v0.1.0

- Super Mario World: Donut Plains 1 secret exit now leads with the Yoshi jump from the pipe (jump with Yoshi, then off his back), with green blocks and Cape as alternatives.
- Launcher: a yellow pixel star appears next to any guide with every checklist item checked off. Grouped guides (Wario Land 4) show a star on each finished difficulty, and on the group once all are done.
- Super Mario World: added the Forest Fortress secret Cape route above the red door, with nine 1-Up Mushrooms and a second door to the Reznors.
- Super Mario World: new "Jump to world" row at the top of the Worlds tab. A (or Select anywhere in the tab) opens a drop-down of all nine worlds with their progress; pick one to jump straight to its first level. The drop-down (and the launcher theme menu) scrolls on shorter screens such as 16:9 devices.
- Super Mario World: rewrote the Valley Ghost House secret exit. The block path comes from a Control Coin you steer with the D-pad into a stair-step pattern (alternating right and up), and the P-Switch is pressed afterwards to turn the coins into blocks; added the go-small tip and the Cape shortcut.
- Super Mario World: removed the "worth confirming" notes (Donut Secret 1, Forest of Illusion 1 and 2, Chocolate Island 3, Yellow Yoshi) after a full playthrough confirmed them.
